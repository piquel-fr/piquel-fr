Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Piquel handles, why, for how long, and who else sees it. It covers the sign-in service at auth.piquel.fr, the account settings and developer console it serves, the website at piquel.fr, and the emails Piquel sends. Apps that let you sign in with Piquel have their own privacy policies, which govern what they do with the data you allow them to receive.

1. Who we are

Piquel is operated by [OPERATOR NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS], the controller of the data described here. Write to support@piquel.fr about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect, and why

We collect only what running your account and keeping it safe needs.

  • Your account. An account identifier, your handle and your display name, and when the account was created and changed. Your handle is public: apps you sign in to receive it, and anyone can learn whether a handle belongs to an account. Handles you no longer use stay reserved, without an owner, so nobody can take them, or a look-alike, to impersonate you.
  • Email addresses. The addresses you add, whether each is verified, and which is primary. We use them to send you security notices, verification and recovery links. An address you don't verify is removed after 24 hours.
  • Passkeys. For each passkey: its public key and credential ID, its signature counter, how it connects (such as USB or internal), whether it is backed up, attestation details that may name the authenticator's model, the name you give it, and when it was added and last used. Your private key never leaves your device or password manager, and we never receive fingerprints, face data or device PINs.
  • Sessions. For each signed-in browser: when and how you signed in, when it was last active and when it expires, its first and latest IP address, and a coarse description of the browser and platform, such as "Firefox on Linux". You see them in your settings, and can sign any of them out.
  • Security history. A record of sign-ins and changes to your account, such as passkeys added or removed, with the IP address and browser of the request. It lets you, and us, spot and undo misuse. Changes made by a Piquel administrator also record who made them and their stated reason, which you see in your history.
  • GitHub and Google. Only if you choose to sign in with them. We ask GitHub for read:user user:email and read your GitHub account ID, name, login and verified primary email address. We ask Google for openid email profile and read your Google account ID, name and verified email address. We keep the provider, your account ID there and the email address, to recognise you next time. We don't read your repositories, contacts, profile picture or anything else.
  • Apps you use. Which apps you allowed, what each may receive, and the tokens that keep you signed in to them. We store tokens and secrets only as one-way digests.
  • Apps you register as a developer. Each app's name, type, redirect and logout addresses, allowed origins, scopes and settings, and digests of its secrets.
  • Invitations, recoveries and deletions. When an invitation was created and used, the progress of an account recovery, and when a deleted account will be erased. Links in these are stored only as digests.
  • Abuse prevention. Short-lived request counters, keyed by a one-way hash of your IP address (and, for emails, of the address), to limit how often anyone can try to sign in or send mail.
  • Service telemetry. Traces, metrics and logs of the requests Piquel serves: the method, route, status and timing. Our collector removes query strings, request and response headers and client IP addresses before storing them.

We don't sell your data, show ads, profile you, or use your data to train models. We ask for no birth date, phone number, address or payment details.

3. Cookies

Piquel uses only cookies it needs to work, so it asks no consent for them. They are set by auth.piquel.fr, and by admin.piquel.fr for Piquel's administrators, cannot be read by scripts, and are sent only over HTTPS. piquel.fr sets none, and there are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

  • __Host-piquel_session: keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days.
  • __Host-piquel_ceremony, __Host-piquel_federation, __Host-piquel_federation_pending, __Host-piquel_restore: the state of a sign-in, a GitHub or Google sign-in or link, or an account restoration in progress, for up to 10 minutes.
  • __Host-piquel_notice: a one-time message on the next page, for up to a minute.
  • __Host-piquel_dashboard and __Host-piquel_dashboard_state: Piquel administrators' dashboard session (30 days) and sign-in state (10 minutes).

4. Legal bases

  • Contract: to provide your account, sign you in to the apps you choose, and send the emails your account needs.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep accounts and the service secure, prevent abuse, keep a tamper-evident record of changes, and run and fix the service. We keep this to what these purposes need.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.
  • Your choice: linking GitHub or Google, and allowing an app, happen only when you ask, and you can undo them at any time.

5. What apps you sign in to receive

An app receives only what its scopes ask for and, unless it is one of Piquel's own apps, what you approve on the consent page:

  • Always: your account identifier, which is the same for every app, when and how you signed in, and an identifier of your session.
  • profile: your handle and display name.
  • email: your primary verified email address.
  • offline_access: to keep you signed in, without more data.

When you sign out, apps you signed in to may be told your account and session identifiers so they sign you out too. Remove an app's access at any time in Settings → Connected apps.

6. Who else sees your data

  • Resend sends our emails, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • GitHub and Google, if you sign in with them, learn that you signed in to Piquel, as with any app using them.
  • Cloudflare routes traffic to Piquel and protects it, so it handles your requests, including your IP address.
  • Our hosting: Piquel runs on servers operated by [HOSTING PROVIDER] in [COUNTRY], with its own database.
  • Piquel's administrators can view an account's settings and history to help its owner and keep the service safe. Every view and every change is recorded, and appears in the owner's security history. They can never add a passkey, a linked account or an email address to your account.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Resend, GitHub, Google and Cloudflare may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own data processing terms and the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. [CONFIRM EACH PROVIDER'S TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS]

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, its email addresses, passkeys, linked accounts and allowed apps: while the account exists. Removed passkeys and linked accounts are kept, marked removed, so your history can still name them.
  • Sessions: up to 30 days, and less once you sign out.
  • Security history: the personal details of each entry (IP address, browser, reasons) for 90 days. What happened and when stays in our tamper-evident audit log until we prune it, without those details.
  • Emails we send: our record of a delivery for 7 days once sent, or 30 days if it failed.
  • Sign-in, link and recovery state, invitations and abuse counters: minutes to a day after they expire or are used.
  • Telemetry: traces for 14 days, metrics and logs for 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and download your data: Settings → Your data downloads everything we hold about your account as JSON, without secrets or key material.
  • Correct it: change your handle, add and remove email addresses and passkeys, and unlink GitHub or Google in your settings. Write to us for anything else.
  • Delete your account: in Settings → Your data. It is signed out everywhere and erased for good after 30 days; until then, signing in restores it. Erasing it removes your profile, addresses, passkeys, linked accounts, sessions, app access and the personal details of your history. What remains: your former handles, reserved without an owner; the audit log's record that events happened, without personal details; and the apps you registered, without their owner, so their identifiers are never reused.
  • Object or ask us to restrict how we use your data, and withdraw anything you allowed.

Write to support@piquel.fr to exercise any right. We answer within a month. You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as [SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY, e.g. the CNIL in France].

9. Security

Passkeys can't be phished or reused on another site. We store tokens, codes and link secrets only as digests, encrypt our signing keys at rest, serve everything over HTTPS, and keep a signed, tamper-evident log of every change to an account. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
