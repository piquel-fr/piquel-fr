# Notifications and email

Piquel tells you what happens to your account in your notifications, and by
email. This page shows where to read them, and how to choose which emails you
receive.

## Before you start

You need a piquel.fr account. Emails go to your primary email address, which
must be verified: add or confirm one in **Settings**, under **Email addresses**.

## Read your notifications

1. Open `auth.piquel.fr/settings/notifications`, or select **Notifications** in
   the settings sidebar. The sidebar counts the unread ones.
2. Select a notification to read it. Opening it marks it read.

A notification about your account's security, such as a passkey a recovery
added, also shows the entry it comes from in your security history, with
**This wasn’t me** if you don't recognize it. **Mark all as read** marks every
notification read, and **Mark as unread** on a notification's page marks it
unread again.

## What Piquel emails you

Every notification is emailed too, in one of two categories:

- **Security:** everything about your account itself, such as a removed address,
  a recovery, or a change Piquel support made. You always receive these, so
  nobody can hide one from you.
- **Announcements:** news from Piquel, such as a change to the privacy policy.
  You receive these by default, and can turn them off.

Some announcements are **essential**: a security incident, or a change to the
privacy policy or terms. You receive those by email even with announcements
turned off. Either way, every announcement stays in your notifications.

## Turn announcement emails off

From your settings:

1. Open **Notifications**.
2. Under **Email**, select **Turn off** beside **Announcements**.

From an announcement's email, select **Stop receiving announcements by email**
at its end, then **Stop announcement emails** on the page it opens. Your mail
app may also offer an **Unsubscribe** button beside the sender, which does the
same in one click.

Turn them back on with **Turn on**, in the same place in your settings.

## Check that it works

The **Email** section says **Not emailed** beside **Announcements**, and your
security history lists **Announcements: emails turned off**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The unsubscribe page says "This link doesn’t work".** The link was cut off
  or changed. Open it from the email again, or turn announcements off in your
  settings.
- **You still received an announcement.** It was essential, or its email had
  already left when you turned them off.
- **You receive no email at all.** Your account has no verified primary
  address. Add one under **Email addresses**.
